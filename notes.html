The buyer will pay for the service to reduce lost hours due to mechanics having to constantly stop work to answer customer queries. This will prevent customers getting frustrated and cancelling future business. It allows customer queries to be answered by checking the job on the computer monitor, which will have a live update feed as work is filtered from the original booking information. The small business will use it to cover everyday information transfer system between customers and the car part providers.
